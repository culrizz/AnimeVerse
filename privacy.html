Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account on AnimeVerse, we collect:

  • Username and email address (when you register)
  • Profile information you choose to provide (avatar, bio)
  • Your watchlist and favorites

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Save your watchlist, favorites, and preferences
  • Improve our website experience

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

AnimeVerse uses local storage to:

  • Remember your login session
  • Save your theme preference (dark/light mode)
  • Store your watchlist and favorites locally

You can clear this data anytime through your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Jikan API — Provides anime data from MyAnimeList. No personal data is sent to this service.
  • Google Fonts — Loads fonts for display. Google may collect usage data per their privacy policy.
  • Font Awesome — Loads icons via CDN.

5. Advertising

We may display advertisements through third-party ad networks. These networks may use cookies to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your browser settings or visit optout.aboutads.info.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

AnimeVerse is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.

9. Contact
