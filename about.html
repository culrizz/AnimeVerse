About AnimeVerse

Your ultimate destination for discovering, exploring, and tracking anime.

What We Offer

AnimeVerse brings together everything an anime fan needs:

  • 🔍 Browse & Search — Discover thousands of anime titles
  • 📊 Track Your Progress — Watchlist, favorites, and completed lists
  • 👥 Characters & Voice Actors — Explore your favorite characters
  • 📰 Anime News — Stay updated with releases and announcements
  • 🖼️ Wallpapers — Download high-quality anime wallpapers
  • 🌙 Dark/Light Mode — Browse comfortably day or night

Our Data Source

AnimeVerse is powered by the Jikan API, an unofficial MyAnimeList API. All anime information, images, and metadata are sourced from MyAnimeList.

Technology

Built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No frameworks — just fast, clean code. Hosted on GitHub Pages for reliable, free hosting.
